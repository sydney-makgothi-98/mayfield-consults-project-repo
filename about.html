About The Gorilla Pit

More than a gym. A community built on hard work, support and real results.

Our Story

The Gorilla Pit started in 2018 as a small garage gym with a handful of barbells and one big idea: training should be tough, welcoming and fun.

Today we're a full-size training facility with a free-weight floor, a boxing area, a spin studio and a yoga room, but the same community spirit still drives everything we do.

Whether you're walking into a gym for the first time or preparing for competition, you'll find coaches who care and members who'll cheer you on.

What We Stand For

Strength for Everyone

Every age, every size, every level. If you want to get stronger, you belong here.

Real Coaching

Our coaches don't just count reps. They teach, correct and help you understand your training.

Community First

We celebrate every win, big or small, and look out for each other on and off the gym floor.

No Egos

Nobody is judged here. Leave your ego at the door and bring your best effort instead.
