Spin

Climb, sprint and ride to the beat in our indoor cycling studio, with lighting and music designed to keep you pushing.

Spin is low impact and easy on the joints, but you control the resistance, so every ride can be as tough as you want it to be.

Duration

45 min

Intensity

Medium – High

Best For

Cardio & endurance

What to Expect

  • A bike set-up check from your instructor
  • A warm-up ride to get the legs going
  • Intervals of climbs, sprints and steady efforts
  • A cool-down and stretch off the bike

Who It's For

Anyone looking to build cardio fitness without putting stress on the knees. Great for runners, cyclists and beginners.
