Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a joining fee?

No. There are no joining fees and no admin fees on any of our plans.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. Monthly memberships can be cancelled anytime with 30 days' notice. Annual memberships are paid upfront and run for the full year.

Do I need any experience?

Not at all. Every new member gets a free fitness assessment and a starter plan, and all classes can be adjusted to your level.

Can I try the gym before joining?

Yes. Book a free trial session through our contact page, or pick up a Day Pass at reception.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, trainers, a water bottle and a towel. Lockers are available in the changing rooms.

Are group classes included?

All group classes are included in the Monthly and Annual plans. Day Pass holders can join one class per visit.
